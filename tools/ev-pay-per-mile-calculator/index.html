EV pay per mile calculator (eVED, UK)

For drivers of electric, hydrogen and plug in hybrid cars. Put in your mileage and see what the new Electric Vehicle Excise Duty could cost each year and each month, next to the Fuel Duty a similar petrol car pays. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Quick answer: from 1 April 2028 the government plans to charge 3p a mile for fully electric and hydrogen cars and 1.5p a mile for plug in hybrids, on top of normal car tax. At 10,000 miles a year that is £300 for an electric car and £150 for a plug in hybrid. A 45 mpg petrol car pays about £535 in Fuel Duty over the same miles at today's 52.95p a litre. Source: GOV.UK.

Proposed, and may change at the Budget on 28 October 2026 eVED is not law yet. The rates and the start date come from the government's published plans and could change at the Budget on 28 October 2026 or before April 2028. From 2029 to 2030 the rate is due to rise each year with inflation (CPI). The DVLA will run it alongside car tax: you give an odometer reading, estimate your miles for the year ahead, and the next reading settles any difference. How eVED is planned to work

Who pays it and when does it start?

Registered keepers of fully electric, hydrogen fuel cell and plug in hybrid cars, from 1 April 2028. The planned rates are 3p a mile for electric and hydrogen cars and 1.5p a mile for plug in hybrids, rising with CPI from 2029 to 2030.

Source: GOV.UK: Electric Vehicle Excise Duty (eVED)

How will my miles be counted?

When you tax the car you give an odometer reading and estimate your miles for the coming year. You can pay up front or spread the cost, and the next reading settles the difference. The DVLA runs it with car tax, and MOT tests already record mileage. Cars under three years old will not need extra mileage checks.

Source: GOV.UK: eVED consultation response

How is the petrol comparison worked out?

Litres used = miles ÷ mpg × 4.54609. Fuel Duty = litres × the duty rate. At 45 mpg and 52.95p a litre that is about 5.35p a mile, so an electric car on 3p a mile pays a little over half.

Source: GOV.UK: amended Fuel Duty rates 2026 to 2027
