Calculators

Free calculators for UK money questions: CIS tax for construction trades, Inheritance Tax, probate and gifts, interest on late invoices, the VAT threshold, window cleaning price rises, stretched funded childcare hours, allotment costs and Christmas collection slots. They run in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Each one links to the GOV.UK rules it uses. They give estimates, not tax, legal or financial advice. Check anything that matters with HMRC or a qualified adviser.
