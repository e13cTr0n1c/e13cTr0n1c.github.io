How it works

The yearly hours are divided by the weeks you are open and rounded down to two decimal places. Many local authorities pay on a 38 week basis, so the hours to claim for each funded term week are the weekly hours times your open weeks, divided by 38.

Rounded down so the weekly hours never add up to more than the yearly hours. Some local authorities round to the nearest hundredth instead. For example, 1,140 hours over 49 weeks is 23.26 hours a week here, because 23.27 × 49 is 1,140.23.

Term by term, the hours for the term are divided by the weeks you are open that term, again rounded down.

Good to know

  • 15 hours a week over 38 weeks is 570 hours a year. 30 hours a week is 1,140.
  • Funded hours must be free to parents. Meals, consumables and activities can be charged only as voluntary extras.
  • If a child leaves part way through a stretched year, they may have had fewer funded hours than were claimed. Say how you handle this in your terms.

Questions

How many hours is 30 hours funding stretched over 51 weeks?

1,140 divided by 51 is 22.35 hours a week, rounded down.

How many hours is 15 hours funding stretched?

570 divided by 51 weeks is 11.17 hours a week; over 48 weeks it is 11.87.

What do I claim for a stretched child?

Many local authorities pay on a 38 week basis: weekly hours times open weeks, divided by 38. Check yours.
