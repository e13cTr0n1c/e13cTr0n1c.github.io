How much more will I repay while the Plan 2 threshold is frozen?

Put in your salary now and the pay rise you expect each year. I work out your Plan 2 repayments for 2027/28 to 2029/30 with the threshold frozen, and next to them what you would pay if the threshold had kept rising with RPI. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Quick answer: You repay 9% of your income above the Plan 2 threshold. The threshold is £29,385 in 2026/27 and Budget 2025 froze it at £29,385 for three years from April 2027, so it stays there for 2027/28, 2028/29 and 2029/30. Sources: GOV.UK Budget 2025, GOV.UK repaying your student loan.

An estimate, not advice I use one yearly salary for each tax year and the yearly threshold. Real repayments come out of each payslip against a weekly or monthly threshold, so your figures will differ a little. I do not model interest, your balance or when the loan is written off. The figures I use

  • Plan 2 threshold £29,385 and repayment rate 9% in 2026/27. GOV.UK repaying your student loan: what you pay.
  • The Plan 2 threshold is frozen at £29,385 for three years from April 2027, which covers 2027/28, 2028/29 and 2029/30. Budget 2025 says: "The repayment threshold for Plan 2 student loans will be frozen at £29,385 for three years from April 2027." GOV.UK Budget 2025. Budget 2026 refers back to the freeze in its financial transactions table. GOV.UK Budget 2026.
  • The 9% rate for 2027/28 onwards. To be confirmed GOV.UK gives 9% now and the Budget did not change it, so I keep it. Budget 2026 annex A of the overview of tax legislation and rates has no student loan figures. Budget 2026 OOTLAR annex A.
  • RPI defaults of 3.6%, 4.3% and 3.0%: the OBR forecast for 2026, 2027 and 2028 in Table A.1 of its October 2026 outlook. OBR Economic and fiscal outlook, October 2026.
  • Plan 1 £26,900, Plan 4 £33,795, Plan 5 £25,000 at 9%, and the Postgraduate Loan £21,000 at 6%, all for 2026/27. GOV.UK repaying your student loan: what you pay. Their 2027/28 thresholds are To be confirmed.

A worked check

On £35,000 now with a 3% rise, pay is £36,050 in 2027/28. With the threshold frozen you repay 9% of £6,665, which is £599.85. If the threshold had risen 3.6% to £30,443 you would repay 9% of £5,607, which is £504.63. That is £95.22 more in the first year. Over the three years you repay £2,094.48 instead of £1,487.52, so the freeze costs you £606.96.

Figures checked on GOV.UK on 31 October 2026.
