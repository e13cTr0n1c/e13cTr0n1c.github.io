    How it works

    For each crop we multiply the amount you picked by the price you entered, then add them up. We add any money you made from selling produce and take away what you spent. If the answer is zero or more, the plot paid its way.

    Tips

    • Weigh as you pick. A cheap hanging scale on the shed door makes it easy.
    • Count one off kit like a new fork in the season you buy it, then it is paid for.
    • The planner has a harvest log page if you prefer to keep the record on paper.
