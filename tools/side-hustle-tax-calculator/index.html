How much tax will I pay on my side hustle?

My trading allowance checker tells you whether you need to report side income. This one goes the next step: the actual tax. Put in your salary and what the side hustle brings in, and I show the extra Income Tax, the Class 4 National Insurance and how much to put aside. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Quick answer: take the £1,000 trading allowance or your actual costs off your takings, whichever leaves less profit. That profit sits on top of your salary for Income Tax, so a basic rate earner pays 20% on it. Class 4 National Insurance is 6% of profit between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above, worked out on the side profit alone. Sources: GOV.UK: trading allowance, GOV.UK: Income Tax rates, GOV.UK: National Insurance rates.

How it interacts with your salary

  • Your salary uses the Personal Allowance first. If your salary is over £12,570, every pound of side profit is taxed at your top rate: 20%, 40% or 45%. GOV.UK.
  • Watch £100,000. Over £100,000 your Personal Allowance falls by £1 for every £2, so side profit there can cost 60% in Income Tax. GOV.UK.
  • Class 4 ignores your salary. It is worked out on your self employed profit, so a side profit under £12,570 pays none. GOV.UK.
  • Class 2. With profit of £7,105 or more in 2026/27 (£6,845 in 2025/26), Class 2 is treated as paid, so it costs nothing. Below that you can choose to pay £3.65 a week (£3.50 in 2025/26). GOV.UK, GOV.UK rates.
  • Payments on account. If your Self Assessment bill is £1,000 or more and less than 80% of your tax is taken through your tax code, HMRC will usually ask for payments on account on top. My payments on account calculator shows the January and July amounts.
  • From 6 April 2027 the trading allowance rises to £3,000, but that is 2027/28, outside this tool. Budget 2026 OOTLAR section 1.5.

The figures I use

  • Trading allowance £1,000 in 2025/26 and 2026/27, or actual costs, not both. GOV.UK.
  • Personal Allowance £12,570, tapered over £100,000. Basic rate 20% on the first £37,700 of taxable income, higher rate 40% up to £125,140, additional rate 45% above. Same in 2025/26 and 2026/27. GOV.UK.
  • Class 4: 6% from £12,570 to £50,270, 2% above, in both years. GOV.UK.

A worked check

Salary £30,000, Etsy takings £6,000, costs £800. Costs leave £5,200 of profit and the allowance leaves £5,000, so I use the allowance. That £5,000 is taxed at 20%, £1,000. No Class 4, as £5,000 is under £12,570. Put aside £1,000.
