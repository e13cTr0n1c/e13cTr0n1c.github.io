Small business rate relief for shops, cafés and pubs from April 2027

For retail, hospitality and leisure businesses in England. Put in your rateable value and I show the small business rate relief percentage you could get in 2026 to 2027 and in 2027 to 2028, plus the pub, social club and music venue relief. I show percentages only, not a bill, because I do not guess multipliers. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Quick answer: From 1 April 2027 retail, hospitality and leisure properties in England get 100% small business rate relief up to a rateable value of £18,000 (now £12,000), tapering to nothing at £21,000 (now £15,000). Relief for pubs, social clubs and most live music venues rises from 15% to 35%. Sources: GOV.UK Budget 2026 policy costings, items 19 to 21, GOV.UK: small business rate relief.

The figures I use

  • 2026 to 2027: 100% relief up to £12,000 rateable value, going down gradually to 0% at £15,000, for a business using one property. GOV.UK: small business rate relief.
  • From 1 April 2027, for qualifying retail, hospitality and leisure properties in England: 100% up to £18,000 and tapered relief up to £21,000. Budget 2026 policy costings, item 20.
  • The taper between £18,000 and £21,000 runs in a straight line, like today's. To be confirmed The costing says the taper applies but does not give its shape.
  • Pubs, social clubs and live music venues: relief rises by 20 points from 15% to 35% from 1 April 2027. Venues with a rateable value of £1 million or more do not get the rise. Budget 2026 policy costings, item 19.
  • Relief for live music venues at £1 million or more in 2027 to 2028. To be confirmed The costing mentions 15% in 2026 to 2027 and a real terms freeze after, but not a percentage for 2027 to 2028.
  • Bill rises for qualifying retail, hospitality and leisure properties are capped by CPI inflation in 2027 to 2028 and 2028 to 2029, which applies to all qualifying RHL properties, not only the ones outside these reliefs. It does not cover rises from improvements or lost reliefs. Budget 2026 policy costings, item 21.
  • How small business rate relief and pub relief combine on one bill. To be confirmed

A worked check

A café with a rateable value of £16,000 gets no small business rate relief in 2026 to 2027, because it is over £15,000. From 1 April 2027 it is under £18,000, so it gets 100%. A shop at £19,500 is halfway through the new taper, so it gets 50%. Today's GOV.UK example matches the same method: £13,500 gets 50%.
