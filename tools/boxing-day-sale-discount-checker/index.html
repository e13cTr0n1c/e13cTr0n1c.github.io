Is this Boxing Day deal a real saving?

Sale labels are designed to make the saving look as big as possible. I check four things: the price after a % off, the real % off from was and now prices, what a multi buy really costs for the number you need, and whether the deal beats the price you could have paid anyway. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Quick answer: a saving is only real against a price you would actually have paid, for something you would buy anyway. If the item sold for less a few weeks ago, or you only need one of a 3 for 2, the true saving is smaller than the label says.

    How I read a sale label

    • Compare with the lowest recent price, not the was price. A was price can be the highest the item has ever been.
    • Multi buys only save if you use them. A 3 for 2 on something you need one of costs you more.
    • Add delivery and returns. A cheaper price with a paid delivery or a short return window can come out worse.
    • Plan the total first. My Christmas budget planner helps you set a limit before the sales start.
