How HMRC usually collects the tax

Your State Pension is taxable, but tax is not taken off before it is paid. HMRC usually changes your tax code so the tax due is collected from another pension or from earnings. If it cannot collect the tax that way, it may send a Simple Assessment after the end of the tax year. That often applies when the State Pension is someone's only income. See How your State Pension is taxed on GOV.UK.

Questions

Is the State Pension taxable?

Yes. Your State Pension is taxable income, but tax is not taken off before you get it. You only pay Income Tax if your total taxable income, including the State Pension, is higher than your Personal Allowance.

How does HMRC collect tax on the State Pension?

HMRC usually changes your tax code so the tax due is collected from another pension or from earnings. If it cannot collect the tax that way, it may send a Simple Assessment tax calculation after the end of the tax year. That often applies when the State Pension is someone's only income.

Will the full new State Pension go above the Personal Allowance in 2027/28?

The Personal Allowance is £12,570 and is frozen. The full new State Pension in 2026/27 is £241.30 a week (£12,547.60 a year), just under the allowance. The expected full new State Pension from April 2027 is £13,036 a year, which would sit above the allowance. That £13,036 figure is expected from ONS inputs and is not yet confirmed on GOV.UK as a 2027/28 rate.

Do pensioners pay National Insurance?

No. Once you are over State Pension age you do not pay employee National Insurance on earnings. This calculator only looks at Income Tax.
