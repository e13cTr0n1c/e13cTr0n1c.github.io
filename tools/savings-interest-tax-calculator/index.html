Quick answer: from 6 April 2027 savings interest above your allowances is taxed at 24% for basic rate taxpayers, 44% for higher rate and 49% for additional rate, up from 20%, 40% and 45%. The Personal Savings Allowance stays at £1,000 (basic) and £500 (higher), the starting rate for savings stays at 0% on up to £5,000, and the ISA allowance stays at £20,000. Source: GOV.UK Budget 2026 rates and allowances.

This is an estimate, not advice. "Tax on interest" is the extra income tax your interest adds, so it includes any Personal Allowance you lose above £100,000. It does not cover dividends, Scottish rates, Marriage Allowance or the new Pension Tax Allowance.

The figures I use

  • Savings basic, higher and additional rates: 20%, 40%, 45% in 2026 to 2027; 24%, 44%, 49% in 2027 to 2028.
  • Starting rate for savings: 0% on up to £5,000 in both years.
  • Personal Savings Allowance: £1,000 basic rate, £500 higher rate in both years. Additional rate taxpayers get none.
  • Personal Allowance £12,570, cut by £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000. Basic rate band £37,700, additional rate over £125,140.
  • ISA subscription limit: £20,000 in both years.

Sources: GOV.UK: Budget 2026 Annex A, rates and allowances, GOV.UK: Budget 2026 overview of tax legislation and rates, section 1.1, GOV.UK: Budget 2026, chapter 3.9.

How the starting rate shrinks as you earn more

The £5,000 starting rate band is cut by £1 for every £1 of taxable pay or pension above your Personal Allowance. On £12,570 or less it is the full £5,000; at £14,570 it is £3,000; from £17,570 it is gone. Any Personal Allowance your pay does not use covers interest first.

A worked check

Pay of £30,000 and £2,000 of interest: you are a basic rate taxpayer, the first £1,000 of interest is covered by the Personal Savings Allowance, and the other £1,000 is taxed. That is £200 in 2026 to 2027 and £240 in 2027 to 2028.

Pay of £14,000 and £6,000 of interest: £1,430 of pay is taxable, so £3,570 of starting rate band is left. £3,570 of interest is at 0%, £1,000 is in the Personal Savings Allowance, and £1,430 is taxed: £286 now, £343.20 from April 2027.
