How much will my State Pension and benefits go up in April 2027?

I built this to show what your State Pension, PIP and other benefits are expected to be from April 2027, using the latest official inflation and earnings figures. There is also a quick check on whether your pay rise beats inflation. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Important These are estimates. The full new State Pension of £250.70 a week (£13,036.40 a year) from April 2027 was confirmed by the government on 28 October 2026, a triple lock rise of 3.9% (earnings growth of 3.9%, ONS, May to July 2026). The other figures are expected until DWP publishes its 2027 to 2028 benefit rates. Most disability and carer benefits use CPI of 3.5% (ONS, September 2026). Official figures are rounded by the government and may differ by a few pence. Not financial advice.

Questions

How much will my State Pension go up in April 2027?

The government confirmed on 28 October 2026 that the full new State Pension goes up by 3.9% to £13,036.40 a year from 6 April 2027, which is £250.70 a week, up from £241.30. That follows the triple lock, the highest of earnings growth (3.9%), CPI inflation (3.5%) or 2.5%. The full basic State Pension is expected to rise by the same 3.9%, from £184.90 to about £192.11 a week, but DWP has not published that exact figure yet.

How much will PIP go up in April 2027?

PIP normally rises by CPI inflation for the September before, which was 3.5% in September 2026. The higher daily living rate would go from £114.60 to about £118.61 a week and the higher mobility rate from £80.00 to about £82.80. The lower rates would be about £79.38 and £31.36. This is expected until DWP publishes its 2027 to 2028 benefit rates.

Is my pay rise above inflation?

Compare your rise with CPI inflation, which was 3.5% in the 12 months to September 2026. If your pay went up by more than 3.5%, it beats inflation and you are better off in real terms. Put your old and new pay into the second calculator to see the real gain or loss in pounds and percent.

How much will Universal Credit go up in April 2027?

I have not guessed this one. The Universal Credit standard allowance is getting a separate rise above inflation, and I could not find the April 2027 figure in the Budget documents published on 28 October 2026. I will add it once DWP publishes it.

Why does the State Pension use the triple lock but PIP uses CPI?

The government has committed to raising the new and basic State Pension by the triple lock. Most disability and carer benefits, such as PIP, DLA, Attendance Allowance and Carer's Allowance, go up by CPI inflation for the September before. Any protected payment on top of the new State Pension also rises by CPI.

When do the new rates start?

The new rates start in April 2027, from the first full payment week after the start of the new tax year. Your first payment at the new rate may be split between old and new rates, depending on your payment day.
