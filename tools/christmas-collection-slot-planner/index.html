How it works

  • Each slot is as long as you choose. One person can hand over as many orders as fit in a slot at your minutes per handover.
  • Multiply by the people on the counter and you get the most orders per slot.
  • Spread your expected orders across the days, and the planner shows how many each slot would need.

Tips

  • Time a few handovers on a normal Saturday to get your own minutes per handover.
  • Give the last day fewer orders if you close early.
  • Keep a few slots free for the customer who forgot to order.
