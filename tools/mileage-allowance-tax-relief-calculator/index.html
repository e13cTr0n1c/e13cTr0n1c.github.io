How the rates work

What are the approved mileage rates?

For cars and vans the tax free rate is 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in the tax year, then 25p above that in 2026/27, rising to 35p from 6 April 2027. Motorbikes are 24p a mile and bikes 20p a mile, with no change at 10,000 miles.

Sources: HMRC approved mileage rates, Treasury everyday fixes announcement, MoneySavingExpert

What if my employer pays less than the approved rate?

You can usually claim Mileage Allowance Relief on the unused balance. Relief is the shortfall multiplied by your marginal income tax rate. A basic rate taxpayer with a £1,500 shortfall would claim £300.

Source: HMRC: vehicles you use for work

What if my employer pays more?

Anything above the approved amount is taxable. Your employer should put the excess through payroll or report it on a P11D.

Does this cover the self employed?

Yes. Under simplified expenses you can deduct the same flat mileage rates from your profits instead of claiming actual vehicle costs. Switch the mode above to see the deduction.
