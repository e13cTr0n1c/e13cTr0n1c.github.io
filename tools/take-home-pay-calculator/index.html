How it works

Salary sacrifice comes off your pay first, so Income Tax, National Insurance and student loans are worked out on what is left.

With relief at source you pay 80% of the contribution from your take home pay and the provider adds the other 20%. If you pay tax above 20%, you can claim the rest back from HMRC, so we show that separately. National Insurance and student loans are worked out on your full pay.

The Personal Allowance of £12,570 falls by £1 for every £2 of adjusted net income over £100,000. Student loans are 9% of pay over your plan threshold, and a Postgraduate Loan is 6% over £21,000.

Questions

Is salary sacrifice better than relief at source?

Salary sacrifice also saves National Insurance and can lower student loan repayments, so take home pay is often higher. Change the setting to compare.

Does this work for Scotland?

Yes. Choose Scotland and the six Scottish Income Tax bands are used. National Insurance and student loans are the same across the UK.
