What will Christmas cost me, and what do I save each week?

I find Christmas creeps up in small amounts. Put in how many people you are buying for and the rest of the costs, and I add it up, compare it with what you want to spend and show how much to put aside each week until 25 December. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Quick answer: add up gifts (people times what you spend on each), food, drink, travel, decorations, cards and nights out. Take off anything already saved and divide by the weeks left. If you are over budget, the planner shows what each gift would need to drop to.

Ways I keep it in check

  • Agree a limit early. A family spend limit or Secret Santa cuts the gift line more than anything else.
  • Book travel now. Trains and coaches over Christmas tend to rise as the date gets closer.
  • Check sale prices properly. My Boxing Day sale checker shows whether a deal is a real saving.
