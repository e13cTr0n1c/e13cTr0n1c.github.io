What software do I need for MTD, and what is bridging software?

You need HMRC recognised software. If you like your spreadsheets, bridging software can connect them to HMRC.

Checked on 2 November 2026

A guide, not advice. I wrote this from GOV.UK guidance checked on 2 November 2026. It is a guide and an estimate, not tax advice. HMRC decides, so check anything that matters with HMRC or an accountant.

Questions and answers

What software do I need for MTD for Income Tax?

Compatible software that can keep digital records of your self employment and property income and expenses, send your quarterly updates and submit your tax return by 31 January the following year. Quarterly updates must go through HMRC recognised software.

Source: GOV.UK: Choose the right software for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax and GOV.UK news, 2 November 2026: Five things you need to know ahead of the MTD quarterly deadline

What is bridging software?

Software that connects to records you already keep, such as spreadsheets, and makes your submissions to HMRC. GOV.UK says some bridging products can send quarterly updates and submit your tax return.

Source: GOV.UK: Choose the right software for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Can I use more than one product?

Yes, but only one product for each separate submission. A landlord cannot use two products to send the property quarterly updates. If you mix products, they need to work together.

Source: GOV.UK: Choose the right software for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Is there free software?

GOV.UK says free products are available for simple tax affairs, though they may have limits such as a cap on transactions.

Source: GOV.UK: Choose the right software for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

How do I choose?

Use the HMRC software finder linked from GOV.UK. Everything listed has been through HMRC recognition, but HMRC does not recommend any product. Check it covers all your income sources and your update periods.

Source: GOV.UK: Choose the right software for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax
