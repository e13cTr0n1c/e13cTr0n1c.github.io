Do I need MTD for Income Tax if I earn under £50,000?

Earning under £50,000 does not mean you are out of Making Tax Digital for good. It depends on your qualifying income and which tax year HMRC looks at.

Checked on 2 November 2026

A guide, not advice. I wrote this from GOV.UK guidance checked on 2 November 2026. It is a guide and an estimate, not tax advice. HMRC decides, so check anything that matters with HMRC or an accountant.

Questions and answers

Do I need MTD for Income Tax if I earn under £50,000?

Not from 6 April 2026, but possibly later. GOV.UK says you need it from 6 April 2027 if your qualifying income was over £30,000 in the 2025 to 2026 tax year, and from 6 April 2028 if it was over £20,000 in the 2026 to 2027 tax year.

Source: GOV.UK: Find out if and when you need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

What counts as qualifying income?

Your total self employment and property income before expenses, which is your turnover, not your profit. Employment pay, partnership profit shares, dividends, the State Pension and private pensions do not count.

Source: GOV.UK: Work out your qualifying income for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Can you show me an example?

GOV.UK gives £25,000 of rent plus £27,000 of self employment income, which makes qualifying income of £52,000. So two smaller incomes can take you over a threshold together.

Source: GOV.UK: Work out your qualifying income for Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Will HMRC tell me?

HMRC checks your Self Assessment tax return and writes to you if you are over the threshold. If no letter arrives, GOV.UK says it is still your responsibility to check and sign up.

Source: GOV.UK: Find out if and when you need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

Are there exemptions?

Yes, for example if you are digitally excluded. If you are exempt you still send a Self Assessment tax return. Exemptions announced in Budget 2026 still need secondary legislation, so treat those as to be confirmed.

Source: GOV.UK: Find out if and when you need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax
