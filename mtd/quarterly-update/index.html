What is an MTD quarterly update?

A quarterly update is a short summary your software sends to HMRC every three months. It is not a tax return.

Questions and answers

What is an MTD quarterly update?

A summary of your self employment and property income and expenses, totalled by your compatible software and sent to HMRC every three months for each business. GOV.UK says updates are summaries, not tax returns, and no payment is due when you send one.

Source: GOV.UK: Send quarterly updates and GOV.UK news, 2 November 2026: Five things you need to know ahead of the MTD quarterly deadline

What is in it?

Totals for each income and expense category, using the same categories as Self Assessment. HMRC does not get your individual receipts or invoices. Each update covers the tax year so far, from 6 April.

Source: GOV.UK: Send quarterly updates

When are the deadlines?

7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May (the following tax year). The second update for 2026 to 2027 is due by Saturday 7 November 2026, and the Self Assessment helpline is open that day from 9am to 3pm.

Source: GOV.UK: Send quarterly updates and GOV.UK news, 2 November 2026: Five things you need to know ahead of the MTD quarterly deadline

What if I spot a mistake?

Correct your records and the change shows in your next update, so there is no need to resend earlier ones. You may resend the fourth update before your tax return if you need to.

Source: GOV.UK: Send quarterly updates and GOV.UK news, 2 November 2026: Five things you need to know ahead of the MTD quarterly deadline

What if I send it late?

GOV.UK says HMRC will not apply penalty points for late quarterly updates in the 2026 to 2027 tax year. After that, a late update gets a point and 4 points means a £200 penalty. Late tax returns still get points.

Source: GOV.UK: Send quarterly updates

Do I send one if nothing happened?

Yes. If you had no income or expenses in an update period, you must still send the update.

Source: GOV.UK: Send quarterly updates
